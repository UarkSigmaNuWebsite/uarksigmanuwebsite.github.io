Why Your Gift Matters

The Gamma Upsilon Chapter has developed men of honor at the University of Arkansas since 1904. Alumni support is what carries that legacy forward — bridging the gap between what dues cover and what it truly takes to build ethical leaders. When you give, you invest directly in the next generation of Sigma Nu brothers.

How to Support Gamma Upsilon

Online — Give quickly and securely through our Projects & Events Fund on Legify, the same trusted platform we use for chapter dues. Choose your amount and give in under a minute.

By check — Make checks payable to the Gamma Upsilon Chapter of Sigma Nu and mail them to the chapter address listed on our Contact page.

Employer matching — Many companies match charitable gifts, doubling your impact. Check with your HR department to see if your employer participates.

Questions about giving? Email us at Uarksigmanupres@gmail.com and the right brother will follow up.

Gifts to the chapter operating account are not tax-deductible. If tax-deductibility matters to you, ask about giving through the Sigma Nu Educational Foundation.
