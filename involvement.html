Life at Gamma Upsilon

Involvement

A well-rounded brotherhood — in service, in leadership, and on campus.

Our Mission

To develop well-rounded, honorable leaders who command respect by redefining the standard of excellence — scholastically, socially, philanthropically, and athletically.

Where Brothers Get Involved

The Pillars of Our Chapter

Philanthropy

Philanthropy & Service

Service has been part of Gamma Upsilon for generations — from campus charity events to hands-on work in the Fayetteville community. Brothers raise funds and give their time in support of local causes and Sigma Nu's national philanthropic efforts, carrying the fraternity's commitment to Honor beyond the walls of the house.

Brotherhood

Brotherhood & Social

Our traditions run deep: the White Rose Formal, Christmas Formal, Sadie Hawkins, and function nights with chapters across campus, all anchored by Homecoming each fall when alumni return to Fayetteville. From big events to everyday life in the house, these are the moments that turn a pledge class into lifelong friends.

Leadership

Leadership & Academics

Sigma Nu develops leaders through its national LEAD program, and Gamma Upsilon brothers put it to work — holding roles in student government, campus organizations, and the Fraternity's own chapter offices. Academics come first, and brothers hold one another accountable to a standard of scholarship worthy of the badge.

Athletics

Athletics & Intramurals

Gamma Upsilon competes year-round in University of Arkansas intramurals — flag football, the Relays, and more. Whether chasing a championship or just fielding a team on a weeknight, brothers bring the same competitive edge and camaraderie to the field that defines life in the chapter.
